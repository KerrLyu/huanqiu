## English classroom edition: Market Power — World Tour

https://kerrlyu.github.io/huanqiu/en/

A two-player property trading game for economics classes: play online with a 4-digit room code (no account), pass and play on one device, or against the computer. It has its own board layout, prices and two decks of economics-themed cards (Market and Policy). Saved games and rooms are kept separate from the Chinese version.
